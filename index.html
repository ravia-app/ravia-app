Most people fight this completely alone.

Adult-content addiction is often invisible from the outside, but deeply disruptive on the inside. Many people are trying to change privately, without language for what they are experiencing.

Most blockers are easy to bypass in seconds. In moments of urgency, the decision becomes automatic before awareness has a chance to return.

Therapy is powerful, but for many people it remains expensive, distant, or not yet possible. Ravia is built for the space in between: private, practical support for a conscious next step.

How it works

Built around pause, pattern, and awareness.

Conscious Pause

When you try to turn off protection, Ravia walks you through a short series of questions. Not to stop you by force, but to make the decision a real one.

Pattern Awareness

Your behavior over time reveals when and why you struggle. Ravia shows you your own patterns in your own words.

Reflective Journal

Log your feelings before and after. Over time, your past self starts talking to your future self.
